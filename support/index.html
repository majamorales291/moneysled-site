Before you write

A few things are quicker to fix yourself.

A receipt was read wrong

Open the receipt and use Edit to correct the store, date, subtotal, tax, tip or total. Line items can be edited, added or deleted individually from the same screen. Corrections are saved immediately and flow through to your analytics.

An item landed in the wrong category

Tap the item, choose Edit, and pick a category — or create your own with a colour. To recategorise a whole receipt at once, use Set all categories from the menu in the top right.

A scan failed or came back empty

Receipts read best in even light, laid flat, with the whole receipt in frame and the total visible. If a scan comes back empty or wrong, try it again — moving in closer, or further back so the entire receipt fits, fixes most failures. A second attempt is often all it takes.

If one particular receipt keeps failing, write in and attach the photograph. That is genuinely useful for improving the reader.

Serbian fiscal receipts

Use the QR scanner rather than the camera. Serbian receipts carry a fiscal QR code, and reading that is both faster and more accurate than reading the printed text.

Reporting a problem

Include whatever of this you have. It usually turns a week of back-and-forth into one reply.

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • What you expected, and what happened instead
  • Your phone and OS version, for example iPhone 14, iOS 18
  • A screenshot, if the problem is visible
  • The email address on your account

Your data

You can export your receipts to CSV, edit or delete anything individually, and delete your account and its contents from Profile in the app. For anything the app does not cover — a copy of your data, a correction you cannot make yourself, or a question about what is stored — write in and it will be handled within 30 days.

What is collected and how long it is kept is set out in the Privacy Policy.
