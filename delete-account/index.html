Delete your account

How to delete your MoneySled account, and exactly what happens to your data when you do.

Delete it yourself, in the app

This is the fastest way and takes under a minute. Nothing is queued or reviewed — the deletion happens immediately.

  1. Open MoneySled and sign in.
  2. Tap the person icon in the top right of the home screen to open Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when asked. Your account and its contents are removed straight away.

This cannot be undone. There is no recovery period and no way to restore a deleted account. If you want to keep a copy of your receipts, export them to CSV from the app before you delete.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can't sign in, or you'd rather we did it, write to support@moneysled.com from the email address on the account, with "Delete my account" as the subject.

We'll confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually within a few days. We may ask you to confirm the request from the account's email address, so that nobody else can delete your data.

What is kept, and why

One thing is kept, and it isn't connected to you.

When a receipt is read, MoneySled separately records that an item cost a given price at a given store on a given date — for example "milk, Kroger, $3.49, 2 April 2026". These records carry no link to your account, your email address or your identity, and cannot be traced back to you. They power the price comparison feature for everyone, so they are kept after an account is deleted.

Because those records are not personal data, they are not covered by a deletion request. Nothing in them identifies you.
