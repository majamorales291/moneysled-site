Privacy Policy

What MoneySled collects, why, where it is stored, and how to get rid of it.

Last updated: 20 October 2026
Operated by: Maja Petrovic, Pennsylvania, United States
Contact: support@moneysled.com

MoneySled is a personal budgeting app. You photograph or scan a receipt, the app reads it, and it keeps the resulting numbers so you can track spending, compare prices and store warranty records. This policy explains exactly what that involves.

It is written plainly on purpose. If anything here is unclear, write to support@moneysled.com and ask.

The short version. MoneySled stores the text read from your receipts, not the photographs. Receipt images are deleted from the server as soon as they have been processed. Your data is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used to build a profile of you. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

1Who is responsible for your data

MoneySled is operated by Maja Petrovic, an independent developer based in Pennsylvania, United States, who is the data controller for the purposes of data protection law. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean MoneySled.

No advertising network, data broker or other third party has access to your account. The only outside services involved are the infrastructure providers listed in section 6.

3How receipt images are handled

This is the part people most often ask about, so it is set out step by step.

  • You take or select a photograph of a receipt in the app.
  • The image is uploaded over an encrypted connection to the MoneySled server.
  • The image is passed to a machine-learning model that reads the text and returns structured data: store, date, items, prices and totals.
  • The structured data is saved to your account.
  • The image file is then deleted from the server. It is not archived, not backed up and not retained for training.

Receipt images are never used to train or fine-tune any machine-learning model, and the model provider is not permitted to retain them for that purpose.

Photographs you attach to a warranty record are treated differently, because the point of them is to keep the receipt or document for the length of the warranty. Those are stored in a private storage bucket, readable only through short-lived signed links issued to your account, and deleted when you delete the warranty or your account.

4How your data is used

Your data is used to provide the features you are using, and for nothing else:

  • Reading receipts and storing the results in your account.
  • Showing you totals, category breakdowns and monthly analytics.
  • Tracking budgets you set.
  • Looking up current prices for an item so you can compare what you paid.
  • Reminding you about warranties that are expiring.
  • Sending essential account email, such as password resets.

Your data is not sold. It is not rented, licensed or shared for advertising, and it is not used to build an advertising or credit profile of you.

5Price data retained after deletion

When a receipt is read, MoneySled separately records that a given item cost a given price at a given store on a given date. These price records carry no link to your account, your email address or your identity. They cannot be traced back to you.

They power price comparison, and in future will support price trends and predictions. Because they are not personal data, they are kept when an account is deleted. Were that not the case, one deletion would degrade the feature for everyone else.

To be explicit about the trade-off: if you delete your account, your receipts, items, warranties, budgets and photographs are removed, but anonymous records of the form "milk, Kroger, $3.49, 2026-04-02" remain in the price history. Nothing in those records identifies you.

7Links to other websites

Price comparison shows where an item is sold, and opening a result takes you to that retailer's website in your browser. Once you leave MoneySled you are on their site and under their privacy policy, and they may set their own cookies or record your visit.

MoneySled does not send your account, your email address or anything from your receipts to those sites. The only thing that travels with you is the page you chose to open.

9Your rights and how to use them

You can do all of the following yourself, from inside the app:

  • See your data. Everything stored about you is visible in the app.
  • Correct it. Any receipt, item, total or warranty can be edited.
  • Export it. Receipts can be exported to CSV.
  • Delete part of it. Individual receipts, items, warranties and budgets can be deleted.
  • Delete all of it. Deleting your account removes your account record, receipts, items, warranties, budgets and warranty photographs.

Depending on where you live you may also have statutory rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to receive it in a portable form, and to complain to a data protection authority. California residents have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights; MoneySled does not sell personal information, so there is no sale to opt out of.

To exercise any right the app does not already cover, write to support@moneysled.com. Requests are answered within 30 days.

10Age restriction

MoneySled is not intended for anyone under 16 and is not directed at children. Accounts are not knowingly created for or by people under 16, and the app contains nothing aimed at children.

If you believe someone under 16 has created an account, write to support@moneysled.com and it will be deleted.

11Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted in transit using TLS. Data is encrypted at rest by the database and storage provider. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Warranty photographs sit in a private bucket that is not publicly reachable, and are served only through short-lived signed links tied to your account. Access to production systems is strictly limited.

No system is perfectly secure, and it would be dishonest to claim otherwise. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, affected users will be notified without undue delay, and any applicable authority will be notified as the law requires.

12Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes, such as a new category of data or a new provider, will be announced in the app before they take effect. Continuing to use MoneySled after a change means you accept the updated policy.
